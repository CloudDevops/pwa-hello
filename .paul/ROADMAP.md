# ROADMAP — niktech-smb

Ten hours a week: weekday evenings plus one weekend morning. Phases are gated, not parallel.

## Phase 01 — Foundations · weeks 1–2 · `phases/01-foundations.md`
Entity, address, phone, contracts. Nothing public ships until this is done, because listings
would re-seed the home address currently being scrubbed from people-search brokers.
**Exit:** LLC filed, business address live, Twilio upgraded, demo number provisioned.

## Phase 02 — Productize · weeks 3–4 · `phases/02-productize.md`
Move the phone stack off the Mac, one config per client, spend caps, fail-open, the ten-call
test script. Deploy the site pages. Set up Google Business Profile and the citation set.
**Exit:** a stranger can call the demo number and book an appointment, and nothing in that path
runs in Nick's house.

## Phase 03 — First clients · weeks 5–8 · `phases/03-first-clients.md`
Saturday walk-ins, evening outbound, founding-client offer, install #1.
**Exit:** 1 paying client, install timed, day-14 review and referrals asked for.

## Phase 04 — Distribution · runs alongside 01–03 · `phases/04-distribution.md`
X, LinkedIn and the channel mix. Cheap, unblocked, and independent of the entity — but it serves
the **enterprise and creator lines, not the SMB beachhead**. Local clients still come from doors
and Google. Start with the profile and a 20-post queue built from vault gotchas.
**Exit:** posting daily from a queue, one demo clip live, X profile complete.

## Phase 05 — Repeat · weeks 9–12
Clients #2 and #3, case study with a real number, testimonial video, founding rate ends,
intake→config automation, first $750 Audit sold.
**Exit:** $1,000+ MRR, install under 4 hours.

## Gates — decide on the date, from the number

| When | Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 8 | 3+ real conversations from walk-ins? | Keep walking | Rewrite the pitch, not the volume |
| Week 12 | 3 paying clients? | Scale the motion | Change vertical, keep the product |
| Week 12 | Install under 4 hours? | Take more clients | Fix the SOP before selling more |
| Month 6 | 5+ SMB clients? | Consider a separate brand | Stay on niktechai.com |
| Month 6 | Custom builds outselling the Front Desk? | That's the trap — re-read the ladder | Working as intended |

# Phase 03 — First clients (weeks 5–8)

Mostly Nick's legs. Claude's job is preparation, lists, drafts and the install SOP.

---

## C1 · Saturday walk-in routine — **Nick**, Claude preps the route

The highest-yield activity on the whole plan, and the one most people won't do.

**Friday evening (30 min, Claude):** 15 salons in a 2-mile radius, ring-out tested during business
hours, sorted by who didn't answer. That's the route.

**Saturday 9–11am (Nick):** walk in before they get busy, ask for the owner, 20 seconds, hand over
a card, **let them call the demo while standing there and say nothing.** Don't close — book 15
minutes by phone for a weekday evening.

Script: `strategy/outreach/call-script.md`. Target: 15 doors → 5 conversations → 2 demos → 1
client. **Do four Saturdays before judging it.** The first one will be terrible; that isn't
information.

---

## C2 · Evening outbound — **Claude drafts, Nick sends**

20 emails per session, one vertical, one city. Cadence: day 1 email → day 4 call + voicemail →
day 8 second email → day 15 break-up → stop.

Templates: `strategy/outreach/email-templates.md`. Personalize exactly one line, always something
only a human would notice.

**Measure replies per 20 sent, not sends.** Below 2 replies per 20 the message is wrong — rewrite
it, don't send more.

Also see the skill `~/.claude/skills/niktechai-outbound/` — it already encodes targeting tiers,
trigger events and the batch workflow for the enterprise side; the same rules apply here.

## C3 · The friend with the dealership IT company — **Nick**

Highest expected value per minute on the entire plan, and it costs one lunch. Template E5 in
`strategy/outreach/email-templates.md`. Three asks: how he got the first dealership, a two-way
referral arrangement (AI → Nick for a cut of year one, break/fix → him), and his actual playbook.

**Do not attack dealerships cold behind him.** He owns that channel.

---

## C4 · Founding-client offer — **Nick**

First three clients: **setup free**, in exchange for a case study, a filmed 60-second testimonial
and two referral intros. Say out loud that it's a founding rate and that it ends — otherwise it
becomes the price.

**Never discount the monthly.** It sets the ceiling forever and every later client hears about it.

---

## C5 · Install #1 — **Claude runs the SOP**, target ≤4 hours

1. **Intake form first** — hours, services, prices, FAQs, booking tool, owner's cell, greeting
   preference, escalation rules. Never gather this on a call; the form is what makes it repeatable.
2. Provision number, generate config from template, wire calendar credentials.
3. Voice: stock (Standard) or 2-minute clone (Pro).
4. Run the ten-call test script. Same ten, every time. Keep the results.
5. Forwarding setup with the owner on the phone — 5 minutes in their carrier settings.
6. Handoff: one-page "how it works", the SMS summary format, how to reach Nick, how to turn it off.
7. Day-3 and day-14 check-in emails, templated. **Day 14 is where you ask for the Google review
   and the two referrals**, while it's still novel and they're still delighted.

**Time it.** Cut it every install. If it stays a weekend, the business doesn't scale.

---

## C6 · Film the install — **Nick**

Anonymized. One episode per install. Proof for SMB prospects, proof for enterprise clients and
recruiters, and genuinely interesting to the AI-engineering audience — at almost no extra cost,
because the install was happening anyway. See `strategy/05-creator-track.md`.

**Not before three paying clients.** Content before revenue is a trap; content *about* revenue is
a flywheel.

## The three sentences

1. The AI phone agent already running on your Mac is the product — productize it, host it in the
   cloud, sell it monthly to appointment-based local businesses.
2. Form the LLC and get a business address and phone **before** any directory listing, or you undo the
   data-broker cleanup you just started.
3. The toddler channel is the one thing here that compounds with nothing else; the engineering notes
   you're already writing are the creator play that pays twice.

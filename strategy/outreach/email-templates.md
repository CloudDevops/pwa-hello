# Outreach templates

Rules that apply to all of them:
- Under 90 words. They read it on a phone between customers.
- One personalized line, always — something only a human would have noticed.
- One ask, and make it small. Never "hop on a call to discuss your AI strategy."
- No jargon. Never the words *AI-powered*, *solution*, *leverage*, *transform*, *synergy*.
- The demo number does the selling. Get it in front of them.

---

## E1 — Cold email, day 1 (the missed-call angle)

> **Subject:** called Thursday at 2pm
>
> Hi {FirstName} — I called {Business} Thursday around 2pm and it rang out. Not a complaint; you were
> probably with a client. That's the whole problem I work on.
>
> I build AI systems for large companies during the week. I've built a small version that answers
> your phone when you can't — tells people your hours and prices, and books them in.
>
> You can hear it right now: **{DEMO_NUMBER}**. Call it and ask it something a customer would ask.
>
> If it's useful, I'm around evenings this week.
>
> — Nick, NikTech AI, Corona

*Why it works: the subject line is a fact, not a pitch. The demo removes the need to believe you.*

## E2 — Cold email, day 8 (the cost-comparison angle)

> **Subject:** cheaper than a receptionist, better than voicemail
>
> Hi {FirstName} — following up once.
>
> Most shops your size have two options for the phone: pay someone $2,500+/mo to sit there, or let it
> go to voicemail and lose the booking. There's a third one now — about $350/mo, answers every call,
> books straight into {BookingTool}.
>
> Demo: **{DEMO_NUMBER}**. Takes 30 seconds.
>
> If it's not for you, tell me and I'll stop emailing.
>
> — Nick

*The "tell me and I'll stop" line raises reply rates and keeps you out of spam complaints.*

## E5 — To your friend with the dealership IT company

> **Subject:** lunch? want to pick your brain + a referral idea
>
> Hey {Name} — I'm building out an AI services line on the side of the Microsoft work. Mostly the
> thing I've built that answers a business's phone and books appointments, plus custom automation.
>
> Two things I'd want to talk about: (1) how you actually got the first dealership — I'd rather learn
> it from you than relearn it badly, and (2) a referral arrangement. AI work that lands on your desk
> comes to me for a cut of year one; break/fix and infrastructure that lands on mine goes to you.
>
> Lunch on me, whenever's good.

*This is the highest-expected-value message on the page. Send it first.*

## Voicemail script (30 seconds, day 4)

> "Hi, this is Nick with NikTech AI here in Corona — I sent you a quick email. I build AI systems for
> large companies, and I've got one that answers the phone for shops like yours when you're with a
> client. Easiest way to see it is just to call it: {DEMO_NUMBER}. That's {DEMO_NUMBER}. No pitch,
> just call it and ask it something. Thanks."

*Say the number twice, slowly. That's the whole point of the voicemail.*

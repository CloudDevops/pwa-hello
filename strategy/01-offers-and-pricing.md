# Offers and pricing

The menu is deliberately short. A solo operator with a day job wins on repetition, not range.

Prices are SoCal-market anchor points for a solo specialist with enterprise credentials. Quote five
prospects at these numbers before changing them — if nobody flinches, they're too low.

---

## The ladder

```
   AI Audit  ──►  AI Front Desk  ──►  Custom Build  ──►  Care Plan
    $750           $299–499/mo         $3k–$15k          $250–750/mo
   (paid           (the product)       (upsell only)     (the annuity)
    discovery)
```

Everything starts at the Audit or the Front Desk. Nothing starts at Custom Build.

---

## 1. AI Audit — $750 flat *(paid discovery)*

**What they get:** 90 minutes on-site or on video, then a written 4–6 page findings doc — where AI
actually saves them money, what it would cost, what to ignore, and what to do first. Vendor-neutral,
including "don't buy anything from me."

**Why it's paid:** free audits attract people who will never buy. A $750 cheque is the cheapest
qualification filter that exists. It also flips the dynamic — you're not pitching, you're delivering.

**The close:** "The $750 comes off the first thing you hire me to build."

**Deliver it in one evening.** Template the doc so it's 90 minutes of writing, not a weekend.

---

## 2. AI Front Desk — $349/mo + $995 setup *(the flagship)*

An AI that answers the business phone when they can't, in a voice they choose, that knows their
hours, services and prices, books into their calendar, and texts them a summary of every call.

**Tiers**

| | Starter | Standard | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup** | $495 | $995 | $1,995 |
| **Monthly** | $199 | $349 | $599 |
| Answers + takes messages | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Texts owner a call summary | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Answers questions (hours, prices, services, directions) | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| Books into their calendar | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| Custom voice clone | — | — | ✅ |
| After-hours + overflow routing | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| Bilingual (English/Spanish) | — | — | ✅ |
| Monthly call-insights email | — | — | ✅ |

Pass-through telephony/voice costs are included up to a fair-use cap (say 500 min/mo); overage billed
at cost + 20%. Put the cap in the agreement — an unmetered voice product is how you end up losing
money on your best customer.

**Anchor the price against what they already pay:** a part-time receptionist is $2,500+/mo loaded.
An answering service is $150–400/mo and takes messages badly. You are cheaper than a person and
better than an answering service. Say exactly that.

**Standard is the one you sell.** Starter exists to make Standard look reasonable; Pro exists to make
Standard look cheap. Most will land on Standard, which is the goal.

**Sell it by demo, not by deck.** "Here's a number. Call it right now, ask it something a customer
would ask." That is the entire pitch and it takes 30 seconds.

## 3. Custom Build — $3,000–$15,000 fixed *(existing clients only)*

Booking systems, review-request automation, quoting tools, intake forms, internal dashboards, agent
setups (OpenClaw/Hermes), document/knowledge assistants.

**Non-negotiable terms, every time:**
- Fixed scope in writing, fixed price, 50% up front.
- Source code and accounts handed over at delivery. They own it.
- 30-day bug warranty. After that it's a Care Plan or it's nothing.
- Change requests are a new quote, not a favour. Write this down before you need it.

**Never quote hourly.** Hourly punishes you for being fast with AI, which is your entire advantage.

**Rough band:** simple automation $3k · booking/intake system $6–8k · multi-part internal tool $12–15k.

---

## 4. Care Plan — $250–$750/mo *(the annuity)*

Hosting, monitoring, updates, prompt/content changes, a monthly check-in email, and a response SLA
you can actually hit. Attach it to every Custom Build at delivery, while they're happy.

**SLA you can keep with a day job:** "Acknowledged within 4 business hours. Non-urgent changes within
3 business days. Outages worked the same evening." Do not promise a phone number.

---

## Things to say no to

- **Hourly work.** See above.
- **Break/fix IT and on-site desktop support.** Daytime, low margin, no leverage. Refer it out — and
  build the referral relationship, because it flows both ways.
- **Websites.** Everyone asks. It's a race to the bottom against Wix and it eats weekends. If a
  client needs one, refer it and take a finder's fee.
- **Anything requiring you to be reachable 9–5.**
- **"Can you just look at our whole IT setup?"** That's an Audit. Quote it.

---

## Discounting policy

Don't discount the monthly — it sets the ceiling forever and every subsequent client hears about it.
Discount the *setup fee* instead, and only in exchange for something:

- Case study + a filmed 60-second testimonial → 50% off setup.
- Two written referral intros → 50% off setup.
- Pay 12 months up front → one month free (this is real cash flow, take it).

**First three clients:** setup free, in exchange for the case study and testimonial. You need proof
more than you need $995. Say explicitly that it's a founding-client rate and that it ends — so it
doesn't become your price.
